GCI 1986 Set Commissioned Young Farmers' Club & ICT Project X

Under the leadership of Set Chairman Seun Esho, the 1986 Set successfully commissioned the Young Farmers' Club (YFC) & Farm Project alongside ICT Project X and Google-supported Career Fairs for GCI students.

Jogor Center, Ibadan Gala Night

40th Anniversary Gala Dinner & Awards Night Celebrated in Style

Old Boys gathered at Function Hall, Jogor Center for an unforgettable evening of brotherhood, awards presentation by Charles Olayinka, and live house song performances.

Venue: Function Hall, Jogor Center, Ibadan
GCI Campus Games Day

Table Tennis Tournament & Asun Night Rekindle 40-Year Bonds

The 4-day celebration featured intense table tennis matches, chess, scrabble, woodblock, and a lively Asun & Pepper Soup social night at the GCI premises.

Coordinators: Kolade Aladeojebi & Ayo Ajanaku
